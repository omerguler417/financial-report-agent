Planned tool

You will name a company (with its ticker and exchange) or upload its financial reports, then choose the financial indicators you want calculated. Each analysis focuses only on the company you request.

  • Read the company's quarterly and annual reports.
  • Summarise financial performance with document and page references.
  • Extract the inputs needed for your selected indicators.
  • Calculate the indicators in Python, showing formulas, inputs, units and periods.
  • Explain the results and relevant financial risks.
  • Compare the company with its own history where comparable data exists.

Missing or ambiguous information will be flagged rather than guessed.
