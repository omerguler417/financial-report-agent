        Planned tool

        You will name a company (with its ticker and exchange), choose the indicators and your output language, and optionally a timeframe (five years by default). The agents fetch the reports and earnings-call material themselves, starting with the company's investor relations website; anything missing can be uploaded above. The focus is companies listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

        • Agent 1, financial analyst: summarises the written reports with precise references, calculates your indicators in Python, and compares valuation with the company's own history and with three to five suitable peers, each one explained.
        • Agent 2, earnings call and news analyst: analyses the earnings call including Q&A, from the official transcript or by transcribing the recording, and reports news published since the latest quarterly report.
        • Agent 3, report combiner: merges both into one report you read here and download as PDF or Word.
