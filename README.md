We believe finance students and individual investors will save time and better understand a company’s performance through three agents that read its financial reports, analyse its management meeting and recent news, and combine both into one document. The agents will use the LLM’s strengths in reading and summarising complex text in any language, extracting information, and interpreting management commentary to explain results and identify inputs for user-selected financial indicators. This approach breaks if the model invents figures, confuses reporting periods, units or translations, performs calculations incorrectly, or presents unsupported news or claims as facts. We will mitigate these risks by citing source passages, calculating indicators using Python, labelling which agent made each statement, and flagging missing or ambiguous information. We would know it works if at least 95% of extracted figures across ten sample reports match a manually verified benchmark, calculated indicators match independently computed results within a predefined rounding tolerance, every news item and management statement in the final document links to a verifiable source, and users complete a standard analysis task faster than manually.
